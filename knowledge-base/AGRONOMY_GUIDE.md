## 2. Crop Management Guidelines

### Soybean (*Glycine max*)
- **Soil & Climate**: Deep, well-drained Black cotton soils (Vertisols) or clay loams with pH 6.0–7.5. Sensitive to water stagnation.
- **Sowing Window**: Kharif onset (15 June to 10 July) after receiving at least 75–100 mm rainfall.
- **Seed Rate & Spacing**: 25–30 kg/acre (bold-seeded varieties like JS 335) at 45 cm x 5 cm row-to-plant spacing.
- **Critical Stages for Moisture**: Flowering (35–45 DAS) and Pod Development (55–70 DAS). Drought during pod filling causes shrivelled grains.
- **Major Pests/Diseases**: Spodoptera litura (tobacco caterpillar), Girdle beetle, Yellow Mosaic Virus, Leaf Blight (Alternaria).
- **IPM Protocol**: Pheromone traps @ 5/acre; bird perches @ 15/acre; spraying NSKE 5% or Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 1 kg/ha at egg hatch.

### Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*)
- **Soil & Climate**: Deep black soils with high moisture-retention capacity; optimal pH 6.5–8.0.
- **Sowing Window**: Pre-monsoon or early Kharif (May–June).
- **Critical Stages for Irrigation**: Square formation, peak flowering, and boll expansion. Excess moisture during vegetative growth causes excessive shedding.
- **Major Pests**: Pink bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*), American bollworm, Whitefly, Jassids.
- **Management**: Install Delta pheromone traps @ 8/acre for monitoring PBW moth catch. Destroy rosette flowers manually.

### Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*)
- **Soil & Climate**: Well-drained clay loams or alluvial soils. Cool winter climate (optimal grain filling at 15–22°C).
- **Sowing Window**: Timely sown: 1–20 November; Late sown: up to 10 December.
- **Critical Irrigations**:
  1. Crown Root Initiation (CRI) at 21 DAS (most critical).
  2. Tillering stage (40–45 DAS).
  3. Jointing stage (60–65 DAS).
  4. Flowering/Booting (80–85 DAS).
  5. Milk/Dough stage (100–105 DAS).
- **Diseases**: Yellow Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*), Brown Rust, Loose Smut.

### Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*)
- **Soil & Climate**: Medium to heavy black soils, alluvial silt loams. Excellent drought tolerance.
- **Sowing Window**: Rabi (15 October to 15 November).
- **Water Management**: Requires 1–2 light irrigations only. *Warning*: Never irrigate at peak flowering (causes flower drop). Irrigate at branching (30–35 DAS) and early pod formation (60–65 DAS).
- **Diseases**: Fusarium wilt, Ascochyta blight. Seed treatment with *Trichoderma viride* (5g/kg seed).
